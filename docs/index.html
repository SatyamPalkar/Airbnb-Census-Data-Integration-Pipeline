03 / METHOD & PROVENANCE

Trace every number.

Aggregates + source hashes ↗

Raw → clean → aggregate

The local Python/SQLite pipeline checks dates and positive finite prices, enforces one listing per month, joins explicit LGA codes to Census, and exports anonymous monthly room-type aggregates.

Raw files and the local warehouse remain off this website. The published JSON includes source-file SHA-256 hashes and reconciliation counts.

Read the limits

These are pandemic-era advertised prices, not current market rates or realized revenue. Availability is not a measure of occupancy. The 2016 Census predates the listing snapshots.

The minimum record count applies to rankings, the scatter and comparison observations; the KPI cards and export retain all matching records.

Source: coursework-provided NSW listing snapshots, LGA mappings, and 2016 ABS Census G01/G02 tables. The original listing publisher was not recorded in the source repo; raw files are not redistributed here.
